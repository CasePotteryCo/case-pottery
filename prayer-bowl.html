How to use your prayer bowl

A prayer bowl is a quiet place to keep what's on your heart. Write it down, set it in the bowl, and come back to it.

Getting started

  1. Find a spot for your bowl where you'll see it every day, like a nightstand, kitchen counter or desk.
  2. Keep small slips of paper and a pen next to it.

Each day

  1. Take a quiet moment and write a prayer, a hope, a worry or a name on a slip of paper.
  2. Fold it and place it in the bowl.
  3. Whenever you pass by, pause and pray over what's inside.

Looking back

  1. Every so often, take the slips out and read them again.
  2. Set aside the prayers that have been answered and give thanks for them. Many people keep these in a jar or journal.
  3. Return the rest to the bowl and keep going.

Caring for your bowl

  • Wipe it with a soft, damp cloth.
  • Keep it away from the edge of tables and shelves, since handmade pottery can chip if it falls.
